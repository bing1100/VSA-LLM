## Search caveats

- Search ranking produced false positives; only directly relevant records were retained.
- arXiv metadata can change after revisions or publication.
- The fast-moving 2025–2026 vocabulary/editing literature needs a formal systematic search before making a publication-level “first” claim.
- Absence from this review is not evidence that no related work exists. Novelty language in the proposals is intentionally scoped to the *specific combinations and evaluations* described.
