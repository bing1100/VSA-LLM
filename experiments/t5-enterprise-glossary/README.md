# T5 enterprise glossaries (E8, task C7)

A private glossary of an invented organisation, generated with a fixed seed, and internal documents
that use its terms: the contamination-free track (no pretrained host can know the terms or facts).

## Sources and licence

Everything domain-side is generated by `src/vsa_embed/benchmarks/glossary.py` (this repository's
licence); the general-text half of the training stream is FineWeb-Edu (ODC-By), shard
`sample/10BT/000_00000.parquet` (sha256 `b1ba7b2ce4cb5ea6…`, see `~/data/vsa-llm/DATA_SOURCES.md`).

## Recipe (E8 steps 1–3, 5, 7)

1. **Ontology.** 4,000 terms of 11 types (system, process, metric, policy, document, project, dataset,
   tool, team, department, committee) with invented multi-token names — stems built from syllables and
   checked against WordNet lemmas and the vocabulary of 20,000 FineWeb-Edu documents (236,428 words),
   unique per term — usually followed by a real head noun ("Brainsh Ledger"), and acronym aliases for
   ≈ 30% (an acronym is never a prefix of a real word, a stem or another acronym, because the
   prefix-causal linker does not check the right word boundary). 23 typed relations (is_a, area,
   purpose, status, cadence, tier, owned_by, depends_on, part_of, measured_by, governed_by, replaces,
   uses, produces, reports_to, …); `ontologies/glossary.py` turns them into frames (3,493 atomics).
2. **Corpus.** Wiki/glossary pages, memos, tickets, meeting notes, incident reports, release notes,
   onboarding guides, chat threads and FAQs stating the frame facts in varied templates plus generic
   office prose. Mention rates follow a Zipf law (exponent 1.5 over a random rank order), tuned so the
   training stream has a rare tail (≈ 850 training terms with 1–9 mentions, 1,800 with 10–99, 770 with
   ≥ 100). Training stream: 47.1M domain + 52.9M FineWeb-Edu = 100.0M SmolLM2 tokens (domain fraction
   0.47). Evaluation documents (3.0M tokens) draw half of their focus terms uniformly over terms, which
   over-samples the tail so the held-out and rare strata are powered; strata are always reported
   separately.
3. **Holdout** (fixed by the generator, `holdout_concepts.txt`, sha256 `e7313dcece6d0b78…`): 360 terms
   (10%, stratified by Zipf rank; never a team, department or committee). They appear only in
   evaluation documents; every training clause naming one is dropped, and the leakage audit (no
   held-out or zero-shot stem or acronym as a word in the training text) passed with 0 leaks.
   **Zero-shot terms**: 200 "new terms defined only by frames" appear in no document at all; nothing
   points to them (`synthetic_concepts.jsonl`, sha256 `407388893336b53e…`).
4. **Cardinality and feasibility.** See `runs/v1/report.md`. Verdict: **feasible at ℓ_min = 2** with
   the trainer's default 256 evaluation windows (180 held-out entries / 1,619 spans, 280 rare entries /
   1,201 spans, covered fraction 0.33). The invented names are long in subtokens, so ℓ_min = 1, 2, 3 give
   the same span sets.
5. **Items** (`items/`, hashes in `items/holdout.json`): `glossary_cloze` (multiple choice over frame
   facts, 2 paraphrases; 600 training terms stratified by training frequency + all held-out terms),
   `term_relation_probe` (linear probe on a neutral mention; labels type, area, owner team, status),
   `zeroshot_property` (3 paraphrases → paraphrase consistency) and `zeroshot_entailment` (true vs
   corrupted statement) on zero-shot and held-out terms.

## Build

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python -m vsa_embed.experiments.track_corpus \
  --config experiments/t5-enterprise-glossary/t5.yaml --output experiments/t5-enterprise-glossary/runs/v1
```

≈ 3 min with 3 tokenizer workers, 1.9 GB peak RSS (main process). Large outputs go to
`~/data/vsa-llm/tracks/t5-glossary/v1/` (`train/`, `eval/`, `eval-general/`, `ontology.pt`, `docs/`).

For training, point the trainer at `data.train`, `data.eval` and `data.ontology` there;
`ontology.pt` has the C3 keys plus `heldout_real_entries`, `synthetic_entries`,
`train_frequency_by_min_subtokens`.
