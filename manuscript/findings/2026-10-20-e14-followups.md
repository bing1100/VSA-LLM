## 1. RD-4b: recall only the item's relation, scored against a context-free null (§13)

**Setup:**
- SmolLM2-360M C5, seeds 1–3, plus C0′ s1.
- Tier P's E11 items.
- The PMI null prompt does not read the context.

**Property accuracy:**

| Condition | T4 real chemicals | T5 new words |
|---|---:|---:|
| `none` | 0.614 | 0.190 |
| `implicit:oracle` (frame in the term's vector) | 0.615 | 0.260 |
| `explicit-sym:oracle` (the whole frame as recall lines) | 0.578 | 0.687 |
| **`explicit-rel:oracle` (only the item's relation lines)** | **0.862** | **0.845** |
| `explicit-rel:typeprior` | 0.717 | 0.811 |
| `explicit-rel:stated` | 0.710 | 0.845 |
| `explicit-rel:linker` | 0.661 | 0.468 |
| `explicit-rel-wrong:oracle` (same lines, wrong partners) | 0.528 | 0.171 |
| `definition` | 0.734 | 0.857 |

**Primary contrasts:**

| Contrast | T4 property / paraphrase / entailment | T5 property / paraphrase / entailment |
|---|---|---|
| (a) `explicit-rel:oracle − none` | **+0.248*** / +0.297* / +0.161* | **+0.655*** / +0.481* / +0.284* |
| (b) `explicit-rel:oracle − explicit-sym:oracle` | **+0.284*** / −0.018 (n.s.) / +0.266* | +0.158* / +0.059* / +0.002 (n.s.) |
| (c) `explicit-rel:oracle − implicit:oracle` | **+0.247*** / +0.314* / +0.161* | +0.585* / +0.418* / +0.264* |

On T4 the raw (uncalibrated) choices agree: property 0.733 → 0.961.

**Predictions:**

| Prediction | Result |
|---|---|
| T4: (a), (b) and (c) positive on property | **yes** |
| T5: (a) and (c) positive | **yes** |
| T5: (b) about 0 | **no**: +0.158*. Selectivity helps even with T5's short frames |
| The wrong-partner control below `none` | **yes**: T4 −0.086, T5 −0.018 |
| Whole-frame recall with the plain null above tier P's −0.10 on T4 | **yes**: −0.036 |

**What explains tier P's T4 drop:**
- The null artefact is about two-thirds of the −0.103 drop: with the plain null the drop is −0.036.
- The remaining −0.036 is the long list itself. It is still significant (p 0.0095).

**Stated-only items** (T4: 131 property items per seed; descriptive):
- `explicit-rel:stated` scores 0.977 against `none` 0.749 (+0.228).
- `definition` scores 0.958 (+0.209).
- Recall of the stated facts slightly beats the definition: +0.020, p 0.001.

**C0′ (plain fine-tuning, s1)** shows the same pattern: T4 (a) +0.240, T5 (a) +0.607. Explicit recall does not depend on
the method's training.

**Decision rule (§13): it fires.** (a) and (c) are positive on T4 property.
- The recommended self-learning route becomes **read → store → selective explicit recall**.
- An E13 amendment is drafted for the author (`experiments/e13-learning-cycle/amendment-proposal-2026-10-20.md`). It
  is not queued.

**Caveats:**
- Oracle frames are gold. With realistic readers the T4 gain is +0.10 (type prior) and +0.05 (linker).
- On T4 overall, the definition text (0.734) still beats the type prior's recall (0.717).
- Recall wins clearly only where the reader's facts are right: oracle frames, and the stated subset.

## 2. LG-B verdict format: gate G1′ (§14)

**Gate G1′ fails for both SmolLM2 hosts. The pre-registered alternative is confirmed:** they cannot relay an executor's
verdict in true/false form. The wording of the negation was not the cause.

**Accuracy on true / false items, taxonomy set:**

| Condition | SmolLM2-135M | SmolLM2-360M | Qwen3-0.6B | Qwen3-1.7B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `exec-vsa` (tier P wording) | 0.818 / 0.317 | 0.987 / 0.148 | 1.000 / 1.000 | 1.000 / 0.997 |
| `exec-vsa-verdict` ("the claim … is False") | 0.542 / 0.427 | 0.460 / 0.540 | 1.000 / 1.000 | 0.940 / 1.000 |
| `exec-vsa-conclusion-verdict` (the verdict line only) | 0.053 / 0.902 | **0.003 / 0.998** | 1.000 / 1.000 | 1.000 / 1.000 |

**What the SmolLM2 hosts do:**
- The verdict format moves their answers toward "False", whatever the verdict says.
- With the verdict line alone, SmolLM2-360M answers "False" to 99.7% of the items whose verdict reads "True".
- They answer from surface cues, not by reading the verdict.
- Yet in cloze format, with the same recall, every host scores 1.000 (tier P).

**The Qwen3 hosts are unchanged.** `exec-vsa-verdict − text` is +0.43 to +0.47* (0.6B) and +0.14 to +0.41* (1.7B) at
every depth.

**Reading:**
- The executor-to-model interface for hosts of about 360M parameters must be cloze or answer-copy, not a true/false
  judgement.
- From 0.6B up, the hosts relay the executor in any format tested.

## 3. LG-P on hard problems: the model as search policy (§15)

**Setup:**
- 156 provable problems: proof depth 2–4, decoy chains of depth 4 × 3 per class, 3 or 30 distractors.
- Proofs found within the budget; all policies sound.

| Policy | Budget 10 | 30 | 100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| random | 0.115 | 0.513 | 0.750 |
| bfs | 0.186 | 0.654 | 0.769 |
| sos (set of support) | 0.199 | 0.641 | **0.994** |
| `score`, SmolLM2-135M | 0.179 | 0.615 | 0.756 |
| `score`, SmolLM2-360M | 0.135 | 0.603 | 0.692 |
| `score`, Qwen3-0.6B | 0.147 | 0.609 | 0.756 |
| **`score`, Qwen3-1.7B** | **0.224** | **0.667** | 0.878 |
| `react`, Qwen3-0.6B / 1.7B | 0.103 / 0.103 | 0.551 / 0.558 | 0.718 / 0.679 |

**Primary, `score − random`:**
- **Qwen3-1.7B: +0.109*, +0.154* and +0.128* at budgets 10, 30 and 100.**
- The other hosts gain only at budget 30: +0.09* to +0.10*.
- SmolLM2-360M is worse at budget 100 (−0.058*).

**Secondary:**
- Qwen3-1.7B ties set of support at budgets 10 and 30 (+0.026, n.s.) but trails it at 100 (−0.115).
- `react` is worse than `score` on Qwen3-1.7B: −0.12 to −0.20.

**Predictions:**

| Prediction | Result |
|---|---|
| `score > random` at budgets 10 and 30 for both Qwen3 hosts | **1.7B yes; 0.6B only at 30** |
| About 0 for the SmolLM2 hosts | **no**: +0.09 to +0.10 at budget 30 |
| `score < sos` for the SmolLM2 hosts | **at budget 100 only** (and 360M at 10) |
| `react ≤ score` | **yes** |

**Reading:**
- Once the search is hard, a model *does* guide a sound prover, and the gain grows with host size.
- A 1.7B base model matches a hand-written symbolic heuristic at small budgets, but not at large ones.
- Scoring the candidate steps beats letting the model write the step number (ReAct-style).
- Soundness comes from the tools, as tier P showed.
