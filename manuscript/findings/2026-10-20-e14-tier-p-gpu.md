## 1. RD-4 / RD-7: explicit recall against implicit injection (pre-registration §1)

**Setup:** SmolLM2-360M C5, seeds 1–3 pooled; paired bootstrap; Holm over 9 contrasts per track.

| Contrast | Track | Property | Paraphrase | Entailment |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| (a) `explicit-sym:oracle − none` | T5 new | **+0.312*** | +0.266* | +0.139* |
| (b) `explicit-sym:oracle − implicit:oracle` | T5 new | **+0.241*** | +0.202* | +0.120* |
| (a) `explicit-sym:oracle − none` | T4 held-out | **−0.103*** | +0.120* | +0.110* |
| (b) `explicit-sym:oracle − implicit:oracle` | T4 held-out | **−0.105*** | +0.137* | +0.110* |
| (c) `explicit-sym:stated − implicit:stated` | T4 held-out | −0.060* | −0.173* | −0.092* |

\* Holm p = 0.0045. On T5 every edge is stated, so (c) equals (b).

**Predictions:**

| Prediction | Result |
|---|---|
| (a) and (b) positive on T5 property | **yes** |
| (a) and (b) positive on T4 property | **no**: both significantly negative |
| T4 `implicit:oracle − none` ≈ 0 | **yes**: +0.002 [−0.004, +0.009] |
| `noisy-explicit` falls as the corruption share rises | **yes**: T4 property 0.494 → 0.464 → 0.414; T5 0.470 → 0.408 → 0.336 |

**Decision rule:**
- (b) is not positive on T4 property, so **no E13 amendment is drafted**.
- (a) is not positive on T4 either. The rule says to report this; tier F's stated-only items (RD-4b) come next and need
  the author's go-ahead.

**Secondary:**
- **The store's own decode is as good as the symbolic record.** `explicit-vsa:oracle − explicit-sym:oracle` is within
  ±0.01 and not significant on both tracks; decode recovery is 0.92 on T4.
- **The frame source matters only on T5.**
  - The type prior's frames give the same gain as the oracle's (+0.323 property).
  - The linker's give about half (+0.169).
- **Implicit injection adds a little on top of explicit recall on T5:** `both:oracle − explicit-sym:oracle` = +0.028,
  +0.034 and +0.024.
- **The definition text and explicit recall each win on different tests.**
  - The definition is better on property (+0.14 on T5, +0.22 on T4).
  - Explicit recall is better on paraphrase (+0.09 on T5, +0.06 on T4).
- **Plain fine-tuning (C0′ s1) shows the same pattern:** T5 property +0.336, T4 −0.125. Explicit recall does not depend
  on the method's training.

**Exploratory: why T4 property falls even though the recall states the answer.** The diagnostics file has the tables.
- **The answer is in the recall.** The oracle recall names the gold partner for 399 of 407 T4 property items, and never
  a distractor.
- **The PMI score cancels part of the recall's evidence.**
  - Property items are scored by PMI against a null prompt that reads the same recall context, in which "this" refers to
    the same term.
  - The gap between raw and PMI accuracy grows when a recall is present:
    - T4: 0.12 with no recall, 0.19 with the oracle recall, 0.27 with the type prior's recall;
    - T5: 0.04 with no recall, 0.25 with the oracle recall.
  - So PMI under-credits every explicit condition.
- **Long recall lists hurt T4 even on raw scores.**
  - The 15-line oracle recall (188 tokens) lowers raw accuracy from 0.733 to 0.696:
    - `has_functional_parent` 0.866 → 0.513;
    - `has_role` 0.738 → 0.626;
    - `is_a` goes the other way, 0.708 → 0.764.
  - The 2–4-line type-prior and linker recalls (about 70 tokens) raise raw accuracy to 0.831.
  - The same dilution appeared with the uncapped in-context glossary (findings 2026-10-18 §2).
- **Interpretation:**
  - **For a new term (T5)**, the recall is the only evidence, so the whole frame helps.
  - **For a term the model partly knows (T4)**, a long list of real ChEBI names interferes with what it already gets
    from the name.

**What this means:**
- **Explicit recall is the route for *new* knowledge:** +0.31 on T5, against +0.07 for implicit injection.
- **Recall should be selective.** Give the item's relation lines, not the whole frame. That design point belongs in any
  later E13 amendment.
- **Measurement:** the null prompt of a context condition should not read the context.

## 2. RD-3: choosing the relation, including the ReAct-style reader (pre-registration §2)

**Measure:** stated-edge F1 (precision); Holm over 2 hosts × 2 contrasts per track.

| Reader | T4, SmolLM2-360M | T4, Qwen3-1.7B | T5, SmolLM2-360M | T5, Qwen3-1.7B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| type prior | 0.573 (0.37) | 0.573 (0.37) | 0.916 | 0.916 |
| linker | 0.101 (0.06) | 0.101 (0.06) | 0.518 | 0.518 |
| host | 0.461 (0.35) | 0.461 (0.35) | 0.758 | 0.758 |
| teacher (T4 only) | **0.603 (0.67)** | 0.603 (0.67) | — | — |
| `mc-rel` | 0.446 (0.28) | 0.415 (0.26) | 0.915 | 0.937 |
| `react` | 0.337 (0.19) | 0.320 (0.19) | 0.917 | 0.936 |

**Primary contrasts:**
- **`react − linker`:** +0.236 and +0.219 on T4; +0.399 and +0.418 on T5. All Holm ≤ 0.002.
- **`react − typeprior`:**
  - T4: −0.236 and −0.254 (Holm 0.001);
  - T5: +0.001 (n.s.) and +0.020 (Holm 0.003).

**Predictions:**
- `react > linker` on both tracks: **yes**.
- `react ≥ typeprior` on ambiguous edges: **T5 yes** (0.72 and 0.79 against 0.70); **T4 no** (0.72 and 0.68 against
  0.85).

**Reading:**
- **`react`'s problem is precision, not recall.** On T4 its recall matches `mc-rel`'s (0.66), but its precision is lower
  (0.19 against 0.28): the fuzzy-span step proposes extra partners.
- **A larger host does not help:** Qwen3-1.7B is not better than SmolLM2-360M.
- **A frontier teacher gains only +0.03 F1 over the type prior.**
- **So on real text, relation choice is not the bottleneck; coverage is.** Only 12% of T4 gold is stated (RD-1a,
  stage 1 of proposal §9). On T5 prose, where everything is stated, every scored reader is at 0.92–0.94.

| Host | d1 | d2 | d3 | d4 | d5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SmolLM2-135M | +0.085* | +0.050 | +0.065 | +0.040 | +0.055 |
| SmolLM2-360M | +0.020 | +0.050* | +0.050 | +0.025 | +0.020 |
| Qwen3-0.6B | +0.470* | +0.440* | +0.465* | +0.425* | +0.435* |
| Qwen3-1.7B | +0.160* | +0.420* | +0.405* | +0.425* | +0.430* |

\* Holm p < 0.05.

**Gate G1 fails for both SmolLM2 hosts**, the hosts it was defined on:
- the depth ≥ 3 gains are not significant;
- and `exec-vsa − exec-sym` is +0.033 and +0.049, outside the ±0.03 band. For the Qwen3 hosts it is 0.000 and −0.002.

The prediction ("G1 passes for the SmolLM2 hosts") failed.

**Exploratory: the SmolLM2 failure is a negation failure in the answer format, not an executor failure.**
- **Split by gold label,** SmolLM2-360M with `exec-vsa` scores:
  - 0.987 on true items;
  - 0.152 on false items, whose recall reads "X is not a Y". Given that line, it answers True.
- **On propositional false items** ("no rule shows that …"), it scores 0.937.
- **In cloze format with the same recall,** every host scores 1.000.
- **The Qwen3 hosts read negations correctly:** 1.000 on both labels.

**Across the board:**
- **Multi-hop over text fails; the executor fixes it.**
  - Qwen3-1.7B on rules as text falls from 1.00 at depth 0 to 0.57 at depth 5.
  - With `exec-vsa` it scores 1.00 at every depth.
- **B5, composition chains of k = 2–6:** `exec-vsa − text` is +0.14 to +0.32 (135M), +0.50 to +0.58 (360M), +0.66 to
  +0.85 (Qwen3-0.6B) and +0.77 to +0.87 (Qwen3-1.7B). Qwen3-1.7B scores 1.000 at every k.
- **B7 cloze:** +0.69 to +0.72 on every host.
- **B4, the two samplers:** LP − RP is between −0.04 and +0.002. The sampler does not matter once the executor answers.
- **Controls, on the Qwen3 hosts:**
  - `exec-nobind` loses 0.50: the executor needs binding;
  - `exec-shuffled` loses 0.35–0.37: the model reads the content, not the format.
- **C4, few-example rules:**
  - Qwen3-1.7B learns the rule in context as well as the mapping vector does (0.962 against 0.963).
  - Smaller hosts gain from the executor (SmolLM2-360M +0.38 to +0.61 on inverse and symmetric at k ≥ 3), except on
    sub-relations (≤ 0).
- **C5, edits:**
  - The store's re-derivation propagates on the Qwen3 hosts: +0.62 and +0.92 over the stale store, and +0.30 over an
    edited text.
  - The SmolLM2 hosts do not follow the edited recall (−0.02 and −0.05).

**Gate G2 fails on its first condition.** In C1–C4 the store only ties fuzzy Datalog (LG-A, 2026-10-17). The second
condition, C5 propagation, holds for the Qwen3 hosts.

So the manuscript should not claim "logic in the store" (soft reasoning better than symbols). The supported claim is
**an exact executor with a model-readable interface**.
