**Terminology (novelty check §6.4).** The method's representation is called an **ontology-composed span embedding**: a gated vector, bound by HRR from a concept's *ontology frame* (the set of its relation–filler edges) over a shared, jointly trained dictionary, and added to the input embedding at the last subtoken of a linked span. "Span channel" names the module that adds it (code: `span_channel`). Bare "semantic channel", "VSA channel", "concept channel" and "latent concept injection" are not used. "Retrofit" means *joint fine-tuning into a pretrained host* (full fine-tuning or LoRA), not lexicon-based post-processing of embeddings (Faruqui et al. 2015). CG-VSA stays the placeholder method acronym.

**Status values:**
- **supported**: the evidence named exists, is committed, and meets the pre-registered criterion.
- **refuted**: the pre-registered refutation condition was met.
- **partial**: some parts were met, others failed or were not tested.
- **inconclusive**: the test ran but cannot decide (CI spans the decision boundary).
- **pending**: not yet run.

Synthetic-only support is labelled as such. LLM-graded results are always labelled "LLM-graded estimate". Numbers are from committed run folders. Intervals are 95% Student-t over 3 seeds unless stated. Tables are regenerated by `figures/make_figures.py`.

**Wording rule.** Claim text uses proposal §7. Where [related-work-recheck-2026-10.md](related-work-recheck-2026-10.md) §6.1 proposes a narrower wording, it is shown as *(re-check → …)* and is not yet adopted. For the E9/E10 claims (section D) the novelty check's recommended wording is adopted as the claim text; it only narrows. No claim says "first", "knowledge editing via the ontology rather than weights" or "makes quantized models robust".
